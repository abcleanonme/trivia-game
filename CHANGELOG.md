# Changelog

Player-facing notes for each TestFlight build. The current build's notes are
also in `scripts/testflight-notes.txt` (pasted into TestFlight "What to Test").

## Next build

GROUP MATCHES
- Fixed "Couldn't send your score" in group matches. If you play before it's your turn, your score is saved and joins the group by itself when the turn gets to you.

RATINGS
- After a great round (80%+ right) or a challenge win, Quizio may ask for an App Store rating. Never on your first day, at most once per app version, and at least 120 days apart.

WEEKLY EVENTS
- Holiday events now take over their week all year: Christmas, New Year, MLK Day (Dream Week), Lunar New Year, the Big Game, Valentine's Day, Pi Day, St. Patrick's Day, Easter, April Fools' Day and Earth Day join Halloween and Thanksgiving. Each has its own reward.
- About 600 new questions written just for events. They only appear in their event, so they're fresh when the week comes.
- Regular themes now take turns evenly, so a holiday no longer skips one.

SPEED ROUND
- No more answer spamming. A wrong answer takes 5 seconds off the clock and locks the answers for a moment while it shows you the right one.
- Three misses ends the round, like Survival.
- Long streaks pay more: 10 in a row in a Speed round is worth x3.

## 0.2.0 (10)

FRIENDS
- New Add friend button on the Friends list opens Game Center's Add Friends sheet.
- Remove a friend from their page (⋯ menu) or by long-pressing their row. Removed friends move to a "Removed friends" list at the bottom where you can restore them. Your record with them stays.

CHALLENGES
- Your turn, Waiting, Finished and Archived fold open and closed, and long lists show the first few with "Show all".
- Archive finished challenges one at a time (long-press) or all at once from the ⋯ menu on Finished. Clear old or all finished ones there too.

FIXES
- The "Use" button in the Style shop is full size, like the buy button.

Please test: adding and removing friends, folding and archiving challenges, and the Use button in the Style shop.

## 0.2.0 (9)

QUESTIONS
- Easier mixed rounds: each 13-question round now has at most 2 hard and 5 medium questions, and difficulty tags were redone so "easy" really means easy.
- New categories: Mythology, Cartoons, Comics and Vehicles. More (Food, Space, Human Body, Words, Holidays, Brands) unlock as they fill up.
- Pick several categories at once on Home and in challenge rules.
- 379 new Quizio original questions, plus 128 more fact-checked ones from the backlog.

DAILY AND WEEKLY TABS
- Daily has its own tab: play today's Daily, see tomorrow's category mix and a countdown, a five-week streak calendar, your Daily stats, and every past Daily with its grid (tap one to share it again).
- Weekly has its own tab: this week's event and track, next week's theme and reward, the weeks after that, and your past events with points, best round and rewards.
- Friends leaderboards on both tabs: today and yesterday for the Daily, this week and last week for the event (your best timed round counts).
- Settings moved to the gear on Profile so all five tabs fit.

STYLE SHOP
- 11 new app themes, including Autumn Leaves and Matcha with falling leaves, Sweetheart and Bubblegum Pop with floating hearts, plus Deep Sea, Thunderstorm, Lantern Night and Galaxy.
- 6 new sound packs: Kalimba, Toy Piano, Steel Drum, Bubble Pop, Crystal and Synthwave.
- Player Card: 23 new avatars, 12 avatar colors, 12 borders, 17 backgrounds, 15 emblems, 18 stickers, 4 letterings, 3 card shapes and 6 finishes.
- The shop now lists free items first, then cheapest to priciest, then ones you earn by playing.

FIXES
- Replay picks fit inside the answer rows instead of spilling out.
- The Group match button is readable in dark themes.

Please test: multi-category picks, the new Daily and Weekly tabs, the Style shop, and whether mixed rounds feel easier.

## 0.2.0 (8)

MULTIPLAYER
- Group matches: challenge up to 6 friends at once, or invite more friends into any match (turn it on in Challenge rules).
- Match breakdown: see every question, each player's answer and who got it right.
- Live replay: watch answers come in as they were picked, or tap Skip to step through question by question. Turn Live replay on or off in the breakdown.
- Player Cards in multiplayer: tap a player's picture to see their full card.
- Everyone in a match needs the same version. If someone's behind, you'll see a reminder to update.

SOUND
- Sound effects for answers, streaks, the clock, power-ups and round results.
- Sound packs in the Style shop: Chimes is free, others cost Sparks. Preview before you buy. Turn sounds off in Settings. Sounds follow your silent switch.

THEMES AND SHOPS
- App themes with animated backgrounds, bought with Sparks in the Style shop.
- Power-up shop: buy single power-ups, packs of 3 or a kit.
- Card Studio: customize your Player Card with new avatar colors, lettering, stickers and finishes.

FRIENDS
- Friends tab with leaderboards, your head-to-head record against each friend and recent games.

EVENTS
- Thanksgiving Feast (Nov 23–29): 50 new holiday questions and a Harvest Leaf emblem.

FIXES
- Themes now show on every screen, and the haptics setting works everywhere.
- Flag a bad question during play, then send your flags from Settings > Flagged questions.
- Faster Home and Profile screens.

Please test: group matches with 3+ friends, inviting a friend into a running match, replays, and sound packs.
