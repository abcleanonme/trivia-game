# Quizio Support

Quizio - Trivia with Friends is a paid trivia game for iPhone and iPad with no ads and no in-app purchases.

## Get help

Found a bug, a wrong answer, or have an idea? Open an issue at
<https://github.com/abcleanonme/trivia-game/issues>.

You can also flag a single question inside the app: tap the flag on any question, then send your flagged questions from Settings.

## Common questions

**Can I buy Sparks?** No. Sparks are earned only by playing. There are no in-app purchases.

**How do I play with friends?** Sign in to Game Center in your device's Settings, then open the Friends tab in Quizio to send a challenge or start a group match.

**Will I lose my progress if I delete the app?** Yes. Your progress is stored only on your device. Quizio collects no data. See the [privacy policy](privacy).

**Where do the questions come from?** Our own question bank, plus questions from the [Open Trivia Database](https://opentdb.com) used under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
