Quizio 0.2.0 – what's new

MULTIPLAYER
- Group matches: challenge up to 6 friends at once, or invite more friends into any match (turn it on in Challenge rules).
- Match breakdown: see every question, each player's answer and who got it right.
- Live replay: watch answers come in as they were picked, or tap Skip to step through question by question. Turn Live replay on or off in the breakdown.
- Player Cards in multiplayer: tap a player's picture to see their full card.
- Everyone in a match needs the same version. If someone's behind, you'll see a reminder to update.

SOUND
- Sound effects for answers, streaks, the clock, power-ups and round results.
- Sound packs in the Style shop: Chimes is free, others cost Sparks. Preview before you buy. Turn sounds off in Settings. Sounds follow your silent switch.

THEMES AND SHOPS
- App themes with animated backgrounds, bought with Sparks in the Style shop.
- Power-up shop: buy single power-ups, packs of 3 or a kit.
- Card Studio: customize your Player Card with new avatar colors, lettering, stickers and finishes.

FRIENDS
- Friends tab with leaderboards, your head-to-head record against each friend and recent games.

EVENTS
- Thanksgiving Feast (Nov 23–29): 50 new holiday questions and a Harvest Leaf emblem.

FIXES
- Themes now show on every screen, and the haptics setting works everywhere.
- Flag a bad question during play, then send your flags from Settings > Flagged questions.
- Faster Home and Profile screens.

Please test: group matches with 3+ friends, inviting a friend into a running match, replays, and sound packs.
